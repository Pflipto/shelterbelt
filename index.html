Why Shelterbelt?

On the northern plains, shelterbelts are rows of trees planted to break the wind, protect the land, and give things room to grow. There’s a certain optimism in planting for a future you cannot yet see. Shelterbelt is built in much the same spirit: a place for small experiments, useful things, and the belief that modest beginnings can make a lasting difference.
